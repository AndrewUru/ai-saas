# Building a Reliable Knowledge Base

A focused knowledge base helps your agent answer confidently without inventing missing details.

## Start with trusted sources

- Product and service descriptions
- Pricing, shipping, and return policies
- Frequently asked questions
- Contact and escalation details

Remove duplicate, outdated, or contradictory documents before uploading them.

## Write for retrieval

Use descriptive headings and keep each section focused on one topic. Include the words customers actually use when they ask questions.

```text
Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
```

## Validation checklist

1. Ask five common customer questions.
2. Compare every answer with the original source.
3. Test a question that is not covered by your content.
4. Confirm the agent admits when information is unavailable.
5. Update the source whenever a policy or product changes.

## Keep it healthy

Review high-traffic content monthly and assign an owner to time-sensitive information such as prices, availability, and promotions.
